# R02 Customer account: Web test plan

**Slice:** R02 · Customer account (EC-64). **Decision:** D74 (API repo). Contracts 0.44.0.

## Tests

- `src/pages/customers/CustomerAccountTab.test.tsx` (English and Arabic): the Account tab opened from `?tab=account` shows the balance block (open balance, total due and the formula line), the overdue numbers, the contracts table, the recent payments with their bank reference and what they paid, the returns with their refund method, the purchases from S07's history (`customer_id`, 10 rows) with links to the sales, and the Statement link; a customer without money shows every empty state; the schedule drawer reads the contract, shows the line statuses (paid, overdue with its days late), an earlier version and the late fee; `CONTRACT_NOT_FOUND` is translated; purchases are hidden from a user without `sale.create` or `report.view`; documents of the customer and its contracts are listed and opened through a signed link; the Details tab is the default and the Account tab loads only when chosen.
- `src/pages/customers/CustomerStatementPage.test.tsx` (English and Arabic): the rows with their kind, details (sale of a contract, payment method and reference, void), opening and closing; a sale links to its page; the dates live in the URL and are sent to the server, clearing them reads the whole history; a range the server refuses shows the range message and disables Print; an unknown customer shows not found; Print calls `window.print()` and the print root holds the A4 sheet (shop, customer, phone, rows, no links).
